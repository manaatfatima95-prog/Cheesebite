Official Brand Guidelines

Chesee Bite Resturent

A premium, modern restaurant brand identity centered around the distinctive Cheese Bite visual mark.

1. Primary Logo

Stacked vertical lockup with signature cheese bite symbol, "Chesee Bite" bold mark, and tracked "RESTURENT" label.

2. Horizontal Logo

Optimized for website navigation header, app headers, food packaging labels, and wide banners.

3. Icon Logo (Cheese Bite Mark)

Standalone minimalist cheese-bite symbol. Used as Android app icon, website favicon, and social media avatar.

4. Light Surface Version

High contrast on warm cream / white surfaces for printed menus, takeout receipts, and delivery bags.
